# Quick start: Android

Use this page to see what's planned for Android and what you can build with canvist today.

**Status: coming soon.** The native Android editor isn't available yet. It will be a `CanvistView` with its own `InputConnection`, tested against Gboard and Samsung Keyboard, and published to Maven Central as `io.github.ifiokjr:canvist-android`. It will work in both Views and Jetpack Compose.

## What you can do today

- **Build with Flutter.** [`canvist_flutter`](./flutter.md) runs the full engine on Android today.
- **Process content on your server.** Use the [Rust](./server-rust.md), [TypeScript](./server-typescript.md), or [Dart](./server-dart.md) packages.

Watch [the releases](https://github.com/ifiokjr/canvist/releases) to hear when it ships.
